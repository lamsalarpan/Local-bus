    What is this

    Local Bus is an ambient radio for a sound every Nepali commuter knows: the driver's cassette or phone, wired into a tinny speaker above the windscreen, playing the same handful of songs on loop while the bus grinds up a switchback road. It's the soundtrack of Dashain homecomings on an overstuffed night bus, of a folk tune drifting out over a Pokhara Lakeside street, of a conductor hanging out the door shouting the next stop over the chorus.

    Pick a route above to change what's queued, tap a conductor's pick for a specific song, or just let it play — the bus doesn't really stop.

    Local Bus is a fan-made concept site. Every playlist and song links to real, existing Spotify content — the five routes are public curated playlists (credited below), and audio plays through Spotify's own embedded player. Nothing is hosted here, and all rights stay with the original labels, composers, performers and playlist curators. If you're signed in to Spotify in this browser you'll hear full tracks; otherwise Spotify plays a short preview.
