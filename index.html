    What is this

    Local Bus is an ambient tribute to a sound every Nepali commuter knows: the driver's cassette or phone, wired into a tinny speaker above the windscreen, playing the same handful of songs on loop while the bus grinds up a switchback road. The five instrumental tracks playing on this page are real, public-domain recordings — legally free to host and stream from anywhere, which is why they can just play, instantly, for anyone. The actual songs — Resham Firiri, Dashain Aayo, and the rest — are one tap away on Spotify.
