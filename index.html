    What is this

    Local Bus is an ambient radio for a sound every Nepali commuter knows: the driver's cassette or phone, wired into a tinny speaker above the windscreen, playing the same handful of songs on loop while the bus grinds up a switchback road. It's the soundtrack of Dashain homecomings on an overstuffed night bus, of a folk tune drifting out over a Pokhara Lakeside street, of a conductor hanging out the door shouting the next stop over the chorus.

    Pick a route above to change what's queued in the player below, or just let it play — the bus doesn't really stop.

    Local Bus is a fan-made concept site. Audio plays through Spotify's official embedded player — nothing is hosted here, and all rights stay with the original labels, composers and performers. If you're signed in to Spotify in this browser you'll hear full tracks; otherwise Spotify plays a short preview. Song credits are put together from public label and film listings.
